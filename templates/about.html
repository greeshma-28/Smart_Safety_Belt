ABOUT SAFEBELT

Smart technology for safer lives.

SafeBelt is an IoT-based women's safety system designed to provide quick emergency assistance through location sharing and trusted contacts.

🎯

Project Objective

The main objective of SafeBelt is to provide a discreet and reliable emergency safety solution that can help a user communicate their location to trusted contacts during an emergency.

⚙️

How It Works

When the physical SOS button on the belt is pressed, the microcontroller activates the GPS and GSM modules to obtain the location and prepare an emergency alert.

🔐

Privacy Focused

SafeBelt is designed so that location information is shared only when an emergency is triggered. Continuous location tracking is not required.
